about

Business graduate from Bristol, now relocating to Bangkok. I came to building through the product side: writing the spec, deciding what to measure, and then finding that AI coding agents meant I could ship the thing myself.

What I'm looking for is a product role where the loop is tight: a real user problem, a build, a number, and the next decision made from that number.

how I work

A one-page spec first. What the thing is, who it is for, and the number that will tell us whether it worked.

Then Claude Code builds while I review each plan before it runs. I stay on the product decisions and let the agent stay on the typing.

Every build keeps a log of what the AI got wrong and how it was fixed. The logs are in the repos, next to the code.
