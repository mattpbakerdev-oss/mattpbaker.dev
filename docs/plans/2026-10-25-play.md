# /play: the drivable portfolio

A small low-poly town at `mattpbaker.dev/play`. Visitors drive a chunky car around it, in the
spirit of The Simpsons: Hit & Run, and pull up at buildings that each open one of Matt's
projects. For clients, employers and followers. It should feel crafted, not like a tech demo.

## Constraints

- Static files only, no build step, no backend. Lives in `/play/` in this repo. Nothing else in
  the site changes.
- Three.js 0.170.0 loaded with an import map from
  `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/build/three.module.js`. No other runtime
  libraries. Physics is a small hand-written arcade model (more controllable than a physics
  engine, and nothing extra to load).
- Every model is built in code from primitives (flat-shaded, low poly). No model files. Sound
  is synthesised with Web Audio. Images and video come from files already on the site.
- Smooth on a mid-range laptop and a modern phone: pixel ratio capped at 1.75, one directional
  light with a 1024 shadow map that follows the car, merged or instanced geometry for repeated
  props, under ~150 draw calls.
- Copy: plain British English, active voice, no em dashes. Never invent facts about the projects;
  all project copy below comes from the existing `/work/*` pages.

## Files

```
play/
  index.html        shell: canvas, loading screen, HUD, panels, touch controls, import map
  play.css          all overlay UI styles
  js/main.js        boot, render loop, wiring
  js/input.js       keyboard + touch → { throttle, steer, brake, interact, horn } state
  js/car.js         car model + arcade physics
  js/physics.js     pure functions: collision (circles vs boxes), ground height, ramps
  js/world.js       terrain, roads, buildings, trees, props, built from data
  js/places.js      DATA: every location, its position, model kind and panel content
  js/extras.js      NPCs, signs, hidden collectibles, knock-over props
  js/ui.js          prompt, panels (dialogs), map/fast travel, HUD, toasts
  js/audio.js       Web Audio synth: engine, horn, bumps, chime, optional music
  test/*.test.mjs   node --test on the pure modules (physics.js, places.js validation, input mapping)
```

Run: `python3 -m http.server` from the repo root, open `http://localhost:8000/play/`.
Test: `node --test play/test/`.

## Art direction

Sunny late afternoon in a toy town. Saturated but warm, never neon.

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Sky | gradient `#9fd3ff` overhead to `#ffd9b0` at the horizon, matching fog |
| Grass | `#7cb860`, with darker `#5f9a48` patches |
| Road | `#4a4547`, dashed `#f5f1ee` centre line, kerbs `#d8d0c8` |
| Buildings | candy set: `#e8735a` coral, `#f2c14e` mustard, `#5bb5a2` teal, `#8f7ad8` lilac, `#f5f1ee` cream; roofs darker |
| Interactable marker | amber `#E0A94A` (the site's accent): a glowing ring on the ground plus a floating, slowly bobbing icon above the door, like Hit & Run's mission markers |
| The car | coral body, cream roof, chunky black wheels |

`MeshLambertMaterial` or `MeshToonMaterial`, `flatShading: true`. Soft hemisphere light plus one
warm sun with shadows. Gentle fog hides the world edge. The town sits on an island-shaped plot
with low hills and trees at the rim so there is no visible edge.

Overlay UI (loading screen, panels, HUD) uses the site's own system so the game reads as part
of mattpbaker.dev: Onest from Google Fonts, warm dark panels `#1C1717` with `#F5F1EE` text and
`#B8AFAC` secondary, amber `#E0A94A` as the single accent, 20px radius, hairline borders
`rgba(245,241,238,0.14)`, pill buttons. Wordmark "Matt Baker" in Instrument Serif on the loading
screen only.

## The town

About 240 x 240 units. A ring road with two cross streets, so every place is a short drive from
any other. The car starts in the centre square, facing the welcome sign.

| Place | Model | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Up Lock | A gym with a huge phone on the roof showing a padlock, dumbbells by the door | Up Lock panel |
| Listing Check | A Bangkok-style condo tower beside an estate agent's office with a giant clipboard sign showing "98" | Listing Check panel |
| UGC pipeline | A film studio with a vertical screen on the roof, a clapperboard and a ring light outside | UGC panel |
| Matt's house | A small cream house with a tuk-tuk parked outside and a graduation cap on the post box | About panel |
| Post office | Red post box, little sorting office | Contact panel |
| Building sites x3 | Fenced plot, crane, cones, "Coming soon" board | "Coming soon" panel: "Something new is being built here." |
| Centre square | Fountain, welcome sign "Welcome to Mattsville. Population: 1, plus whoever's driving" | nothing |

Ramps: three, one in the park, one by the building sites, one on a cross street. Trees, lamp
posts, benches and fences fill the gaps.

## Driving

Arcade, forgiving, fun. Top speed ~28 units/s, reverse ~10. Acceleration eases in, grip is high
with a little drift at speed, steering tightens as speed rises. The car tilts slightly into
turns and pitches on braking. Collisions with buildings, trees and lamp posts bounce the car
back with a thump sound and a small camera nudge (no nudge with `prefers-reduced-motion`).
Ramps launch the car; gravity brings it back and the suspension squashes on landing. Falling
off the plot is impossible (invisible boundary). If the car flips or gets stuck, R resets it
upright on the nearest road.

Chase camera: behind and above, smoothed position and look-at, pulls back a little with speed.

## Controls

- Desktop: WASD or arrows to drive, Space to brake, E or Enter to interact, H for the horn,
  M for the map, R to reset, Esc to close a panel.
- Phone and tablet (any coarse pointer): a virtual joystick bottom-left (up/down throttle,
  left/right steer) and round buttons bottom-right: interact (only lit near a place), horn.
  Map and mute live in the top bar. Respect safe-area insets.
- First-load "How to play" card showing the right controls for the device, dismissed with any
  key or a button. Stored in localStorage (wrapped in try/catch) so it shows once.

## Places and panels

Driving into a place's amber ring shows a prompt: "Press E to look inside" (or "Tap to look
inside" on touch). Interacting stops the car and opens a panel: an accessible modal dialog
(`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, labelled, focus moved in and trapped, Esc and a close button
return focus to the game). Game input pauses while a panel is open. On phones the panel is a
bottom sheet; on desktop a right-side card 440px wide, so the town stays visible.

Every place lives in `js/places.js` as data. Adding a project means adding one object:

```js
{
  id: 'up-lock',
  kind: 'project',          // project | about | contact | soon
  model: 'gym',             // which builder in world.js draws it
  position: [x, z], rotation: 0,
  title: 'Up Lock',
  tagline: '...',
  body: ['paragraph', ...],
  stats: [{ value: '~145', label: '...' }],
  media: [{ type: 'image' | 'video' | 'youtube', src, alt, poster }],
  tech: ['Swift', ...],
  links: [{ label, href }],
}
```

A `soon` entry with no content renders the "Coming soon" panel. `test/places.test.mjs` checks
every entry has the required fields for its kind, unique ids, positions on the plot and no two
rings overlapping.

## Extras

- **Signs**: six or so short, funny wooden signs in Matt's voice, readable when the car is near
  (a floating label fades in). Examples: "Speed limit: whatever ships it", "Caution: specs ahead",
  "This road was built by an AI. I reviewed the plan", "Bangkok: 9,500 km that way".
- **Characters**: four blocky townsfolk who walk short loops on the pavements. A speech bubble
  with one line appears when the car is close. If the car hits one, they ragdoll-hop out of the
  way, say "Oi!", and carry on. Never harmed.
- **Knock-over props**: cones, bins, crates and fence panels. Hit them and they tumble with
  simple rigid-body motion (velocity, spin, gravity, ground bounce) then reset to their spot
  out of view after 20 seconds.
- **Hidden extras**: four collectibles tucked away (behind the gym, on top of a ramp jump, in
  the park, behind the building sites): a golden dumbbell, a film reel, a graduation cap, a
  mini tuk-tuk. Each spins and glows faintly. Driving through one plays a chime and a toast
  with a one-line note (e.g. tuk-tuk: "Bangkok, next. Two weeks' notice and I'm on the plane").
  HUD counter "Found 1 of 4", saved in localStorage. Finding all four shows a small celebration
  and a line from Matt.
- **Horn**: a cartoon double honk. Townsfolk turn to look.

## HUD

Top bar, translucent dark pill: "Matt Baker" wordmark linking to `/` (labelled "Back to
mattpbaker.dev"), the collectible counter, a Map button, a sound toggle (muted by default until
the first interaction; music separate from effects, both in a small sound menu). Bottom centre:
the interact prompt. Minimap top-right on desktop: top-down dots for places, amber for unvisited,
dim for visited, an arrow for the car.

## Map and fast travel

M (or the Map button) opens a dialog: a top-down drawing of the town with each place labelled,
and a list of buttons beneath. Choosing one drops the car outside it facing the door. This is
also the keyboard and screen-reader route to every panel.

## Loading screen

Warm dark background, "Matt Baker" wordmark, "Loading Mattsville" and a thin amber progress
bar. Then a "Start driving" button (needed anyway to unlock audio). A small "Prefer the normal
site? mattpbaker.dev" link.

## Accessibility and motion

- All overlay UI keyboard reachable, visible amber focus ring, labelled buttons, contrast AA.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no camera shake, no bobbing markers (static), no celebration
  particles; driving itself stays.
- Canvas has `aria-label` describing the game and pointing at the Map for a list of places.
- If WebGL is unavailable, show a friendly card with a link to the normal site.
