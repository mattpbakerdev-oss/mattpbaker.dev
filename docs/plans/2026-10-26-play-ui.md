# /play UI upgrade: Hit & Run cartoon overlay

Mattsville's overlay currently borrows the portfolio site's quiet dark system. Matt wants it to
feel like a game. Direction chosen by Matt on 26 Oct 2026: **Hit & Run cartoon**, applied to the
title screen, in-game HUD, project panels and map, with four new pieces: speedometer, objective
tracker, pause menu and arrival banners. Panels get a game frame but the body text stays clean
and easy to read, because recruiters read them.

This supersedes the "Overlay UI uses the site's own system" paragraph and the HUD wordmark ruling
in `2026-10-25-play.md`. Everything else in that spec (accessibility, reduced motion, copy rules,
no invented facts, static files only) still holds.

## Art direction

Chunky, outlined, bright, slightly tilted, bouncy. Like stickers slapped on the screen.

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#1E1424` | Outlines, hard shadows, text on light surfaces |
| `--yellow` | `#FFD23F` | Primary badges, primary buttons, header bands, focus ring |
| `--orange` | `#FF8A1E` | Secondary accents, speedo needle, active states |
| `--cream` | `#FFF4DC` | Panel and card bodies |
| `--sky` | `#3FB6FF` | Info accents (map water/roads highlight, objective ticks background) |
| `--red` | `#E8473A` | Close buttons, warnings |
| `--green` | `#4CC26B` | Completed ticks |

- **Display type:** Lilita One (Google Fonts) for logo, badges, banners, prompts, headings and
  button labels. Upper or title case. **Body type:** Onest 400/500 for paragraphs, stats labels
  and anything longer than a few words, so panels stay readable.
- **Shape language:** 3px `--ink` outline on every HUD element, 14 to 18px radius, hard offset
  shadow `4px 4px 0 var(--ink)` (no blur). Badges and banners tilt -2 to -3deg; nothing that
  holds paragraphs tilts.
- **Motion:** spring-in pops (scale 0.6 to 1 with a small overshoot, 220 to 320ms), slide-ins for
  banners, a press-down on buttons (translate 2px, shadow shrinks to 2px). Under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`: no overshoot, no slides, opacity only; tilts stay static.
- **Contrast:** ink on yellow, ink on cream and cream on ink all pass AA. Never white text on
  yellow or orange. Focus ring: 3px `--yellow` with a 3px `--ink` outer ring so it shows on both
  light and dark.

## Title screen (replaces the loading screen)

- The town renders behind it while loading, with the camera slowly orbiting the centre square
  (static under reduced motion).
- **MATTSVILLE** logo in Lilita One: yellow fill, thick ink outline (layered text-shadow or SVG
  stroke), hard shadow, slight tilt. Under it a small cream tag: "A portfolio by Matt Baker".
- Loading: a chunky fuel-gauge bar ("Fuelling up..."), then the button becomes "Press any key to
  start" on keyboard devices and "Tap to start" on touch, gently pulsing. Any key (except Esc,
  which browsers don't count as a gesture that may start audio), click or tap starts (and
  unlocks audio). The logo is sized by width and height, and the title scrolls rather than clips
  on short screens, so the start button and the normal-site pill are always reachable.
- Small chunky pill bottom: "Prefer the normal site? mattpbaker.dev".
- Then the camera swoops from the orbit to the chase position (instant under reduced motion).

## In-game HUD

- **Top-left:** the Mattsville mini logo badge (desktop only) and the objective tracker card
  underneath. *(Round 2: the collectible star badge is gone; one "x / 4" counter on screen.)*
- **Objective tracker:** a cream card with an ink outline, title "TO DO" in Lilita One, items:
  "Visit Up Lock", "Visit Listing Check", "Visit the UGC studio", "Say hello at the post office".
  Each ticks with a green tick pop when that panel is opened (reuse `ui.visited`). Collapsible:
  a small chevron; collapsed by default on any touch device and on compact screens, showing a
  "To do 1/4" chip. On desktop it starts open, folds to the chip once the how-to card closes,
  and opens for about 3s when a tick lands (unless the visitor has toggled it themselves). A
  fifth, visually optional row "Find the hidden extras" ("Hidden extras", count underneath, on
  the narrowest phones) carries the collectibles with its own
  "0/4" count; it is not part of the four. When all four visits are done, a toast: "All done" /
  "That's the tour. The building sites are for whatever I build next."
- **Top-right:** round chunky icon buttons: Map, Sound, Pause. Below them on desktop, the minimap
  in a thick rounded frame with a little "N" badge (not on short windows, 500px tall or less,
  where the compact speed badge takes that spot).
- **Back to site:** a small pill "mattpbaker.dev" at bottom-left on desktop (always visible, as
  the original spec required) and inside the pause menu on all devices.
- **Speedometer (bottom-right on desktop):** a round dial, cream face, ink outline, orange needle
  sweeping 0 to 70, big Lilita number in mph underneath (convert: 1 unit/s = 2.237 mph, top speed
  shows about 63). Needle eased, not jittery, on its own layer turned by a CSS transform; the DOM
  is written only when the rounded angle or the number changes. On touch devices and compact
  screens, a compact badge "24 MPH" under the round buttons, top right, clear of the touch
  buttons and the banner slot.
- **Prompt:** a tilted yellow card in the arrival banner's top slot on every screen, never over
  the car, and only once the banner has gone (the banner hands over to it in the same place).
  Desktop: one row at the top centre: the place name (26px Lilita One, 23px under 1200px wide),
  then "Press [E] to look inside" (chunky keycap). Phones (portrait or landscape): stacked, in
  the slot under the HUD rows; the lit yellow hand button carries the action. Pops in from 0.85
  scale; the moment it starts to hide it is inert and hidden from assistive tech.
- **Arrival banner:** when the car enters a place's zone (radius 9 round the ring centre, left
  beyond 11) with the place ahead of it (within about 70 degrees of the nose; waived inside 5
  units), a banner slides in: place name in Lilita One on a yellow band with an ink outline, and
  the tagline beneath on a smaller cream band. Desktop: in the sky band, top centre between the
  HUD clusters, clear of the roof props. Phones: the name only, about 30px, in the slot just
  under the HUD rows. Entrance: one keyframe with a fixed small overshoot, about 420ms ease-out.
  Holds 2.5s, slides out. Once in a zone the car stays in it until beyond the leave radius, so
  overlapping zones never flip the banner. Once per approach. Never while a dialog is open.
  Building sites (one zone along the row): "BUILDING SITE" / "Coming soon".
- **Toasts:** cream cards with an icon disc, title in Lilita One, line in Onest; pop in from the
  top-centre and stack under whichever of banner or prompt holds the top slot; polite live
  region kept. Landscape phones: a compact strip (30px icon, title only; the line stays in the
  live region text) so the place ahead stays in view.
- **Touch controls:** the joystick and buttons restyled in the same language (ink outline, yellow
  interact button with the hand icon, cream horn button).

## Panels (project, about, contact, coming soon)

- Frame: cream body, 3px ink outline, hard shadow, 20px radius. Header band in yellow with the
  title in Lilita One (tilted -2deg as a sticker overlapping the band edge is welcome) and a
  round red close button with an ink outline.
- A small kind label above the title: "PROJECT", "ABOUT ME", "GET IN TOUCH", "COMING SOON".
- Body in Onest, ink on cream, 16 to 17px, generous line height. Images keep rounded frames with
  an ink outline.
- Stats as chunky tiles: big number in Lilita One (the number may tilt; tiles holding labels do
  not), label in Onest. Small labels ("Built with", "Email", "Sound") in sentence case, 15px;
  only the kind chip stays in caps. In panels under 480px the link buttons run full width.
- Tech as small outlined pills. Links as chunky buttons: primary yellow, secondary cream, both
  with ink outline and press-down. "(opens in a new tab)" stays for screen readers.
- Desktop: right-side card as now. Phone: bottom sheet as now, same drag-to-dismiss.
- The post office copy-email row restyled to match.

## Map screen

- A paper map: cream card, ink outline, the town drawn in its palette with thick outlines,
  each place a round pin badge with an icon (projects get an app tile, never the collectible
  star), visited pins get a green tick. Ramps in muted tan. Car shown as an arrow badge, drawn
  above the labels. The post office label sits to the right of its building. Title "MAP OF MATTSVILLE" in Lilita One on a yellow band.
- The list beneath as chunky buttons with the place colour dot, name in Lilita One, kind in
  Onest, and a tick when visited. Keyboard and screen reader behaviour unchanged.

## Pause menu (new)

- Opens with Esc or P when no other dialog is open, or the Pause button. Freezes the game like
  the other dialogs (same modal rules: focus moved in and trapped, Esc or Resume closes).
- Dimmed scrim with a slight blur; a centred card with "PAUSED" in Lilita One. Pause and the map
  open quickly with no bounce (opacity .12s, scale .18s ease-out); the HUD hides behind them.
  Esc with the sound menu open closes the menu first; holding Esc never flickers Pause.
- Buttons, stacked: Resume, Map, Controls (opens the how-to card), Sound (effects and music
  toggles inline), Back to mattpbaker.dev.

## How-to card

Restyled to match: cream card, yellow header "HOW TO PLAY", keycaps drawn as chunky keys.
